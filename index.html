How to play

Touch

Touch the left half of the screen to drop a stick under your thumb, and let go to hide it. Attack is the large button in the bottom-right, with four skill petals around it. Tap a skill to cast at your target, or drag off it to aim. A gold + spends a point. Only a learned skill can sit on a slot. The map card in the top-right toggles. Shop uses category tabs and an icon grid.

Mouse and keys

Click the ground to move. Click an enemy to attack. Arrow keys also move. Q W E R cast the four belt skills (Shift+key ranks one up). T opens stats and the loadout. Space attacks. B opens the shop. 5 and 6 drink potions.

Controls

Touch

Touch the left half to anchor a stick under your thumb. Releasing it hides the stick. Tapping the ground does not walk you. Attack is the big button in the bottom-right, with four skills arcing around it. Tap a skill to cast, or drag off the button to aim and drag back onto it to cancel. Potions sit just left of that fan. A gold + spends a point. The map card in the top-right toggles. Points opens stats and the loadout. Shop is a category list and an icon grid. The fight pauses while a panel is open.

Mouse and keyboard

Click ground to move, click an enemy to attack. Arrow keys move. Q W E R cast the four belt slots. Shift plus a belt key ranks that skill. T opens skills and stats. Only a learned skill can be dragged onto the belt. Space attacks. B toggles the shop. G returns you to the fountain if you stand still. 5 drinks a health potion, 6 a mana potion. Esc closes panels.

Win by destroying the Harrogath Worldstone. If the Encampment Worldstone falls, the match is lost. You respawn beside the campfire.
