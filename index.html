How to play

Touch

Stick on the left moves. Attack and skills sit on the belt. Tap the ground to walk, or tap an enemy to chase them. Shop opens anywhere; Akara only sells inside the Encampment. The red orb is life, the blue orb is mana.

Mouse and keys

Click the ground to move. Click an enemy to attack. Arrow keys also move. Q W E R cast skills (Shift+key ranks one up). Space attacks. B opens the shop. 5 and 6 drink potions.

Controls

Touch

Move with the stick. Hold Attack to strike whatever is in reach, or tap an enemy to lock onto them. Skills sit on the belt. A glowing + spends a skill point. Shop always opens; Akara only sells inside the Rogue Encampment, and the panel says so when you are outside. HP and MP buttons drink potions. The red orb is life and the blue orb is mana.

Mouse and keyboard

Click ground to move, click an enemy to attack. Arrow keys move. Q Bash, W Leap, E War Cry, R Whirlwind. Shift plus a skill key ranks it up. Space attacks. B toggles the shop. 5 drinks a health potion, 6 a mana potion. Esc closes panels.

Win by destroying Andariel's altar. If the Rogue Encampment falls, the match is lost. You respawn beside the campfire.
