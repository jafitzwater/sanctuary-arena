How to play

Touch

Stick on the left moves. Attack and skills sit on the right. Tap the ground to walk, or tap an enemy to chase them. Shop at the Encampment. Drink potions with the two small buttons.

Mouse and keys

Click the ground to move. Click an enemy to attack. Arrow keys also move. Q W E R cast skills (Shift+key ranks one up). Space attacks. B opens the shop. 5 and 6 drink potions.

Controls

Touch

Move with the stick. Hold Attack to strike whatever is in reach, or tap an enemy to lock onto them. Skill buttons are on the right. A glowing + spends a skill point. Open the shop inside the Rogue Encampment. HP and MP buttons drink potions.

Mouse and keyboard

Click ground to move, click an enemy to attack. Arrow keys move. Q Bash, W Leap, E War Cry, R Whirlwind. Shift plus a skill key ranks it up. Space attacks. B toggles the shop. 5 drinks a health potion, 6 a mana potion. Esc closes panels.

Win by destroying Andariel's altar. If the Rogue Encampment falls, the match is lost. You respawn beside the campfire.
