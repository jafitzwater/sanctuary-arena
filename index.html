How to play

Touch

The large stick on the left is the only way to move. Tapping the map does nothing. Attack sits in the bottom-right corner, with four skill slots in an arc. Points spends skill and stat points. Drag a skill onto a slot, or tap a skill and then a slot. Shop opens anywhere; Akara only sells inside the Encampment.

Mouse and keys

Click the ground to move. Click an enemy to attack. Arrow keys also move. Q W E R cast the four belt skills (Shift+key ranks one up). T opens the skill tree. Space attacks. B opens the shop. 5 and 6 drink potions.

Controls

Touch

Move only with the large stick on the left. Tapping the ground does not walk you. The big Attack button is in the bottom-right corner, with four skill slots around it. Round HP and MP buttons sit above the arc. Points (the badge turns gold when you have points) spends skill and stat points. Drag a skill onto Q W E R, or tap a skill and then a slot. Life and mana are the bars beside your portrait. Akara only sells inside the Rogue Encampment.

Mouse and keyboard

Click ground to move, click an enemy to attack. Arrow keys move. Q W E R cast the four belt slots. Shift plus a belt key ranks that skill. T opens skills and stats. Drag a skill onto the belt, or click a skill and then a slot. Space attacks. B toggles the shop. 5 drinks a health potion, 6 a mana potion. Esc closes panels.

Win by destroying Andariel's altar. If the Rogue Encampment falls, the match is lost. You respawn beside the campfire.
