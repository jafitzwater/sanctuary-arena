How to play

Touch

The large stick on the left is the only way to move. Attack sits in the bottom-right corner with four skill slots. A gold + on a skill spends a point. Shop uses category tabs and an icon grid. The chip by the HUD is a one-tap buy for the next recommended item.

Mouse and keys

Click the ground to move. Click an enemy to attack. Arrow keys also move. Q W E R cast the four belt skills (Shift+key ranks one up). T opens stats and the loadout. Space attacks. B opens the shop. 5 and 6 drink potions.

Controls

Touch

Move only with the large stick on the left. Tapping the ground does not walk you. Attack is the big button in the bottom-right corner, with four skills around it. A gold + on a skill spends a point. Points opens stats and the loadout. Shop is a category list and an icon grid; tap an icon, then Buy. The recommended item beside the HUD buys in one tap inside camp. Tap the dimmed area or × to close. The fight pauses while a panel is open.

Mouse and keyboard

Click ground to move, click an enemy to attack. Arrow keys move. Q W E R cast the four belt slots. Shift plus a belt key ranks that skill. T opens skills and stats. Drag a skill onto the belt, or click a skill and then a slot. Space attacks. B toggles the shop. 5 drinks a health potion, 6 a mana potion. Esc closes panels.

Win by destroying Andariel's altar. If the Rogue Encampment falls, the match is lost. You respawn beside the campfire.
